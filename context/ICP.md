## Primary ICP — The Embedded AI Customer

**Who they are:**
- HR, L&D, or Enablement leader at a mid-to-large org (~500–10,000 employees)
- Title patterns: VP People · Director of L&D · Head of Enablement · Chief People Officer · CLO
- 10+ years in their function. Operator, not analyst.
- Reports to a CHRO, COO, or CEO. Has budget authority or strong influence.

**What they're feeling right now:**
- Pressure from above to "do something with AI" — usually vague, sometimes panicked
- 77% of L&D leaders say AI ownership inside their org is unclear (research signal)
- Watching tech teams get embedded engineers (FDE postings up 800%+ in 2025) and asking "where's the equivalent for us?"
- Skeptical of vendors. Burned by tools that promised transformation and delivered chatbots.
- Personally curious about AI but doesn't have time to become a builder

**What they actually need:**
- Someone who has built real Software 3.0 systems — not a deck
- Someone who speaks their language (RAPID, ADDIE, Kirkpatrick, ABP) AND speaks builder language (agents, pipelines, orchestration)
- A working artifact they can point at and say "we want that, here"
- A path that doesn't require their team to become engineers overnight

**What makes them buy:**
- A LinkedIn post that says something true about their world they haven't seen anyone else articulate
- A demo they can use in 60 seconds that obviously couldn't have been a wrapper
- A conversation where Gregg diagnoses their actual problem before pitching anything (the Assessment Before Prescription signal)

**Where they hang out:**
- LinkedIn (primary discovery surface)
- ATD, CLO, Josh Bersin community (peer signal)
- Closed Slack groups — Modern L&D, People Tech Partners, Lattice/Gem peer networks
- Newsletters: Josh Bersin, Stacy Harris, Jeff Berger, Chief Learning Officer

**Anti-ICP — do not pursue:**
- Pure HR-tech vendors looking for content help (we are not their content shop)
- Generalist consultants without domain depth (our differentiator is L&D craft + builder craft)
- Enterprise compliance-training buyers (commodity, not Software 3.0)
- Tiny orgs (<200 employees) — not enough surface area for an embedded model

## Secondary ICP — Fellow Builders

**Who they are:**
- Other practitioners watching the build-in-public process
- Mid-career L&D or HR people learning to build agent systems
- Independent consultants exploring an AI practice

**Why they matter:**
- They are not the buyer. They are the **amplifier**.
- They re-share, comment, and recommend. They feed the algorithm and bring the buyer to us.
- Some of them eventually become the buyer when they move into a leadership role.

**What they need from the content:**
- Honest mistakes and pivots, not just wins
- Specific tool stacks and decisions, with the reasoning
- A teachable framework they can use in their own work (this is what curriculum-draft.md becomes)

**Anti-pattern:**
- Don't optimize content for this group at the expense of the primary ICP. They appreciate depth; the primary ICP needs the headline first.

## The Brand Signal

> "That's cool. How did he do that?"

Every published artifact (LinkedIn post, README, demo, landing page) should provoke that response from the primary ICP.

**Test before publishing:** If a tired Director of L&D scrolling LinkedIn at 9pm sees this post, does it stop their thumb? If yes, ship. If no, rework.

## Category Positioning

**Owned category (per analysis-and-synthesis):**
**"Embedded AI Accelerator for HR, L&D & Enablement Teams"**

- Nobody holds this term on LinkedIn (verify quarterly)
- Nobody has built Software 3.0 infrastructure to back it up — except Gregg
- The category bundles three differentiators: L&D craft expertise, real Software 3.0 builds, embedded delivery model

**Companion concepts to seed in content:**
- "The 20% problem" — shipped functionality vs. shipped product
- "Assessment Before Prescription" — Gregg's diagnostic frame
- "Software 3.0 for people teams" — translating Karpathy for the HR audience
- The FDE analogy — "what an embedded engineer is for product, I am for people teams"

## Content That Reaches This ICP (AMPLIFIER reference)

| Content type | Frequency | Primary purpose |
|--------------|-----------|------------------|
| Build post (what shipped this week) | 1/week | Proof of craft — "look what's possible" |
| Lesson post (what I learned) | 1/week | Teachable insight — "here's the principle" |
| Reframe post (industry critique) | 1/2 weeks | Category authority — "the conventional wisdom is wrong because…" |
| Newsletter | 1/week (eventual) | Deep dive — practitioner's guide format |
| Case study | 1/month | Social proof — KCU, ODIN demo, etc. |

**Hard rule:** No video for distribution. Written-first strategy. (Per kickoff prompt and MAPPER constraint.)
